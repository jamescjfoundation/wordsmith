Manuscript is where you write the book. Wordsmith Book is where it gets designed and typeset for
print and ebook. You don't need to reformat anything to move from one to the other.

<Callout type="info">
  Manuscript's side of the hand-off is built: the book package and the Offered fixes inbox work
  today. Wordsmith Book itself is <Status value="in-progress" /> and has no release yet.
</Callout>

## How Book reads your manuscript

Wordsmith Book opens your Manuscript project directly: either the project folder (`.wsm`) or a
packed project (`.wsmz`). It reads the words and the structure (parts, chapters, scenes, front and
back matter) and your book details.

Book **never writes to your project**. Its own work (the design, the editions, print-only and
ebook-only choices) is kept in a separate Book file. So you can go on writing in Manuscript while the
book is being designed.

Some things stay behind on purpose: items you have left out of export, screenplays, story bible
entries and research are not part of the book.

## The book package

File › Export › **Book package** packs the project into one `.wsmz` file for Wordsmith Book. It
is useful when you send the book to someone else to typeset.

Before the file is kept, Manuscript reads the package back the way Book will and checks every
word. The export report lists the book details Book needs that you haven't set yet, such as the
copyright year, the rights statement or a description. Fill them in under File › Book Details.

If you keep [several drafts](./drafts-and-progress.mdx#drafts), **Export This** in View › Drafts
chooses the one that goes to Book.

## Fixes offered back

While typesetting, Book may spot a small mistake: a typo, a missing comma. It doesn't change your
project. Instead it **offers the fix back** to Manuscript.

<Steps>
<Step>

### See what's waiting

The status bar shows how many fixes are waiting. Click it, or run **Offered Fixes** in the command
palette.

</Step>
<Step>

### Decide on each one

Each fix shows the document, the reason, and the words before and after.

- **Accept** takes the fix into your text (and can be undone in one step).
- **Reject** turns it down.
- **Open** shows the document.

</Step>
</Steps>

If you have changed those words since Book made its offer, the fix can only be rejected. Your
decisions are kept in the project, and Book never overwrites your words without showing you the
difference first.
