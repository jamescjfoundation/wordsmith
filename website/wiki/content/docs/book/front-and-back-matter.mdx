A printed book has pages before and after the story: a title page, a copyright page, a contents
page, an "about the author" page. Wordsmith Book makes these for you from your book's details, so
you don't have to type them and keep them in step by hand.

## The outline

<Status value="available" />

The **Outline** panel on the left shows the whole book in reading order:

- **Front matter**: the half title, title page, copyright page, dedication, contents and so on.
- **The body**: parts, chapters and the scenes under them.
- **Back matter**: about the author, "also by", acknowledgements.

Click any item to see it in the middle of the window. Pages that Book makes can be dragged to a
new place. Chapters and scenes from a linked Manuscript project stay in the writer's order: change
that in Manuscript.

## The pages Book makes

A new book starts with the usual pages: half title, title page, copyright page and contents at
the front, and about the author and "also by" at the back.

| Page | What goes on it |
| --- | --- |
| Half title | The title alone. |
| Title page | Title, subtitle, the name the book is published under (the pen name if there is one), and the imprint. |
| Copyright page | The copyright line, the rights statement, the edition's ISBN with its format ("paperback", "ebook"), the imprint and its address. |
| Dedication and epigraph | Your own words, set apart and centered. |
| Contents | Parts and chapters, with page numbers in print and links in the ebook. |
| About the author | Your text about yourself. |
| Also by | Your other books, grouped by series. |

The copyright page and "also by" are filled in from templates with placeholders such as
`{title}`, `{byline}`, `{year}`, `{holder}`, `{isbn}` and `{imprint}`. If a template uses a
placeholder Book doesn't know, it is left as written and Book tells you.

If a linked manuscript already has its own copyright page or dedication, Book uses the writer's
page in its place and never adds a second one.

In print, the front matter runs in the usual order: half title, "also by", title page, copyright,
dedication, epigraph, contents. The ebook leaves out the half title, which only makes sense on
paper.

## Chapters and parts

<Status value="in-progress" />

Book numbers chapters and parts for you. These settings are built and saved with the book, and
are waiting for their place in the window.

- **Chapter number styles:** "Chapter One", "Chapter 1", "One", "1", "I", or no number. You can
  change the label word too, for example "Book" in place of "Chapter".
- **Parts** are numbered on their own and get a part page with their number and title.
- **Each chapter** can have a subtitle and an epigraph with its source, or be left unnumbered.
- **A chapter can be print-only or ebook-only**, for pages that only make sense in one of them.

If a chapter's title is already a number ("Chapter Twelve"), Book doesn't add a second one.

## Your book's details

<Status value="available" />

The **Details** panel shows what Book knows about the book: the title, the name it is published
under, the subtitle and the language. For a linked book these come from the manuscript.

Under **Still needed**, Book lists what is missing before the book can be published, such as an
ISBN for an edition or a trim size for a print edition. Some of these block export, and some are
only warnings.

Book can also keep details that belong to publishing rather than writing: contributors with their
roles (editor, translator, illustrator and others), the imprint's name, address and website,
subject codes, and the copyright holder. These are kept in the book project and never written to
a linked manuscript. Setting them in the window is still to come. <Status value="in-progress" />

## Editions

<Status value="available" />

One book can have several **editions**, each its own product with its own ISBN. In the Details
panel, choose **Add Paperback**, **Add Hardcover** or **Add Ebook**, then type each edition's ISBN.

Book checks ISBN-13 and ISBN-10 numbers as you type and tells you if the check digit is wrong.
Two editions can't share an ISBN.

An edition also holds its trim size, paper (white, cream or color), price notes and any changes to
the design just for that edition, such as a larger type size. These are kept with the book and
their screens are still to come. <Status value="in-progress" />
