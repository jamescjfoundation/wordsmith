Wordsmith Book is built in steps, in the open. Each step is finished and tested before the next
one starts. This page follows the plan in plain words.

## The first release (0.1)

The first usable release is made of ten milestones. Six are done.

| Milestone | What it brings | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | The app's shell, name, look and sample books | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Project | The book project, linking a Manuscript project, saving, recovery and the outline | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Import | Word, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown and text imports, finding chapters, the word-for-word check and typographic cleanup | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Structure | Front and back matter, chapter and part numbers, the book's details, editions and ISBNs, and fixes offered back to the writer | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Designs | Six designs, mixing them, your own designs, and the design chooser | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Print | Typesetting the whole book, trims and margins, chapter openings and the print PDF | <Status value="available">Done</Status> |
| Ebook | The EPUB, its navigation, accessibility information, fonts and cover, and Book's own EPUB checks | <Status value="in-progress" /> |
| Preview | Print spreads and the ebook preview, updated as you work | <Status value="planned" /> |
| Preflight and export | The checks, the standard EPUB validator, and exporting the PDF, EPUB and project bundle | <Status value="planned" /> |
| Release | The command line, the user guide, installers for macOS, Windows and Linux, and update checks | <Status value="planned" /> |

"Done" means built and tested inside Book. Some of it is still waiting for its screens in the
window: the pages in this section say which.

**How the first release is tested.** Two public-domain novels, one of them over 150,000 words and
one with parts, notes and verse, go from a Word file and from a Manuscript project to a 6 × 9 in
paperback PDF and an EPUB in each of the six designs. Every word of both has to match the source,
the PDFs have to pass preflight, and building twice has to give the same files.

## After 0.1

### 0.2: most novels

<Status value="planned" />

- Re-importing a revised file without losing your design and settings.
- Footnotes at the foot of the page, pop-up notes in ebooks.
- More front and back matter: foreword, prologue, epilogue, afterword, an excerpt from your next
  book and more.
- Store editions: ebook variants with each store's own links.
- Hardcover, bleed, more trim sizes, and a cover template with a check of your cover.
- Warnings for widows, orphans, stacked hyphens and loose lines.
- Page numbers in the ebook that match the print book.
- More interface languages and better screen reader support.

### 0.3: designs, series and standards

<Status value="planned" />

- Twelve to sixteen designs, including large print, and a large-print version of any design.
- Box sets with one main contents.
- Tagged and archival PDFs, and a proof PDF with line numbers.
- Re-designing an existing EPUB.

### 1.0: a small press

<Status value="planned" />

- House styles that lock a design for an imprint or series.
- Imprint profiles, a series view and a list of the ISBNs an imprint owns.
- Book metadata in the standard format distributors use.

### Later

Ideas for after 1.0 include translated editions that share a design, fixed-layout ebooks, a
shared gallery of designs, and turning plays from Wordsmith Script into books.

## What Book won't do

So you know where to look elsewhere:

- **Writing and heavy editing.** That's Wordsmith Manuscript's job. Book only makes small fixes,
  and offers them back to the writer.
- **Cover art.** Book will make the exact cover template and check your cover, but you make the
  art in another app.
- **Uploading.** Book makes the files and a checklist; you upload them to the store or printer.
- **Picture books, comics, cookbooks** and other books with a fixed layout.
- **Academic books** with citations, indexes and cross-references.
