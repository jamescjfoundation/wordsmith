Wordsmith is a family of free, open-source apps for writers. Each app does one kind of writing
well, and they hand work to each other. This wiki explains how to use them, from your first page
to a finished book.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Wordsmith Script" href="./script/index.mdx" icon={<AppIcon app="wordsmith-script" />}>
    Screenplays, TV episodes and stage plays. You type, and Return and Tab do the formatting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wordsmith Manuscript" href="./manuscript/index.mdx" icon={<AppIcon app="wordsmith-manuscript" />}>
    Novels and long work: a binder of scenes, a corkboard, a story bible and drafts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wordsmith Book" href="./book/index.mdx" icon={<AppIcon app="wordsmith-book" />}>
    Turns a finished manuscript into a print book and an ebook. In development.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="warn" title="Early days">
The apps work, but none has been released yet, and there are no installers. To try one today you
build it from its source code, which takes a terminal and a few minutes.
[Installing](./start/install.mdx) shows how.
</Callout>

## About this wiki

The wiki is written in the open, like the apps. Every page has an **Edit on GitHub** link at the
bottom. If something is wrong, unclear or missing, that's the quickest way to fix it, or see
[Getting help](./start/help.mdx).
