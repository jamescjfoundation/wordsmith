# Using the Wordsmith names and logos

Wordsmith's code is open source. Its names and logos are not. They are how a writer knows that an app, a page or a download really comes from the project, so they stay the project's own.

## What this covers

- The names Wordsmith, Wordsmith & Ink, Wordsmith Script, Wordsmith Manuscript, Wordsmith Book and Wordsmith Launcher.
- Every file in this brand kit: the logos, wordmarks, marks, lockups, app icons, palette cards, guides and PDFs, and the scripts in `source/` that draw them.
- The copies of those files, wherever they are kept: `website/assets/brand/`, `website/favicon.svg` and `website/wiki/public/brand/` in this repository, and `assets/brand/` in each app's repository.

The names and logos are trademarks of The James C. J. Foundation, and all rights in the brand files are reserved. The open licenses that cover the rest of this repository (MIT, Apache-2.0, CC BY 4.0) do not cover them. [`LICENSES.md`](../LICENSES.md) lists which terms cover which folder.

The names are working names until the trademark check is done.

## Fine, without asking

- **Naming the project.** Say or write the names to talk about Wordsmith or its apps: in an article, a review, a talk, a tutorial, a list of software.
- **Showing a logo to point to the project.** Use a logo or an app icon, unchanged, to show that you mean Wordsmith: beside a link to [wordsmithand.ink](https://wordsmithand.ink/) or to a repository, in a comparison, in a video.
- **Press.** Use the logos, the icons and pictures of the apps in coverage of the project.
- **Linking.** Link to the site, the wiki, a repository or any file here.

Take the files as they are. Use the `-ink` versions on light backgrounds and the `-paper` versions on dark ones, and leave clear space around a logo. [`suite/BRAND_GUIDE.md`](suite/BRAND_GUIDE.md) has the details.

## Not fine

- **Passing something else off as Wordsmith.** A fork, a changed build, another app, a service or a site may not carry these names or logos, or a name or logo close enough to be taken for them. A fork needs its own name and its own icon.
- **Changing the marks.** Do not redraw, recolor, stretch, crop or add to a logo or an icon, and do not join one to another logo.
- **Borrowing the project's voice.** Do not use the names or logos in a way that says the project or The James C. J. Foundation stands behind you, your product or your opinion.

## Anything else

Ask first. Open an [issue in the wordsmith repository](https://github.com/jamescjfoundation/wordsmith/issues) and say what you would like to do.

## Not covered here

- **The fonts.** The logos are set in Courier Prime and the text in Inter. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows using their letters in a logo. The fonts are their makers', not part of this kit.
- **The code and the words.** The apps' code, this repository's code, and the text of the website and the wiki are under open licenses: see [`LICENSES.md`](../LICENSES.md).
