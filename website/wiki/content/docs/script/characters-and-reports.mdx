## Scene properties

**Scene Properties** shows the scene at the caret: its heading, a title, a
color, the summary, and who is in it. Open it from the **Scene** panel in the
Inspector, from an index card's **Properties** button, or with **Character in
Scene** on the **Insert** tab.

- Speakers are listed for you. You can hide one from the scene's cast, and add
  someone who is there but doesn't speak.
- Write an **arc beat** for each character: what changes for them in this
  scene.
- The color shows on the scene's index card and in the structure strip.

All of it moves with the scene when you reorder scenes.

## Character details

**Characters** on the **Review** tab (or the **Characters** panel in the Script
panel) holds each character's **traits**: Age, Gender, Role and the rest, and
any you add. It also holds a **highlight color**: turn the highlight on and every
speech of that character is tinted on screen. Highlights are never printed.

### Renaming a character

In the **Characters** table (see [Views](./views.mdx#tables)), click a name and
type the new one. Every cue (with its extension, like V.O.), every mention in
action and dialogue, the autocomplete list, the traits and the highlight all
change at once. One undo puts it all back.

## Reports

**Reports** (<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>J</Kbd>, or a tile on the
**Review** tab) opens a window with eight reports. Each can be sorted where it
makes sense and saved as text or CSV.

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Scenes** | Number, heading, title, page and length of every scene; the longest and shortest; the last page. Production order groups scenes by location. |
| **Locations** | Each place with its scenes, intros and times of day. Headings missing a part are listed. |
| **Cast** | Speeches, words, speaking and non-speaking scenes for each character; who speaks most and least. |
| **Character** | One character's scenes (with arc beats), who they share scenes with, their monologues (100 words or more), and every speech if you like. |
| **Statistics** | Words and paragraphs per element, per character and per scene; other scenes at the same place; flagged words with their pages and speakers. |
| **Notes** | Every note in the script. |
| **Filtered script** | Only the elements you pick (all the scene headings and dialogue, say). It can also be saved as a PDF laid out as a script. |
| **Representation** | Characters grouped by two traits, with their share of the characters, the dialogue and the scenes. |

**Character CSV** on the **Review** tab saves a quick character report for a
spreadsheet: speeches, words, scenes, first and last scene, and pages for every
speaking character.

## Writing statistics and goals

**Statistics** on the **Review** tab (or click the word count in the status bar)
shows your writing habits: current and longest streaks, your best day of the
week and hours, and words, pages or minutes by day, week, month or year, with
your goal drawn as a line.

- Set goals in pages a day (on so many days a week) and in minutes a day. The
  **Goals** panel in the Inspector shows how today is going.
- Writing time stops counting after a few idle minutes.
- Statistics are kept on your computer, not in your scripts. You can turn them
  off in **Preferences**.

## Sprints

Click **Sprint** in the status bar (or on the **Review** tab) to write against
the clock: 25 minutes, unless you choose another length in **Preferences**. When
it ends you see what you wrote.
