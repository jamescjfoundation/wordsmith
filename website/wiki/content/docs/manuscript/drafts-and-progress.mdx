## Word counts

The status bar always shows the words in the current document and in the selection, the
manuscript's progress toward its target, and the words you have written this session. The outline
shows each item's words, and its target with progress.

## Targets

File › **Targets** is where you set:

- a word target for the whole manuscript;
- a deadline and the days of the week you write;
- a target for this session.

The status bar then shows your progress and how many words each writing day needs to meet the
deadline. You can also start a timed **sprint** there; the status bar counts down and shows the
words you write during it.

Each item can have a target of its own too, set in the inspector.

## Writing history

**Writing History** (on File › Targets, or in the command palette) shows a calendar of the words
you wrote each day, darker for busier days, with the month's total, your best day and your current
streak. Deleted words count against the day, so the numbers stay honest.

Your history is yours alone. It is kept on this computer beside the app's preferences, never in
the project, so it doesn't travel with the project or show up for anyone you share it with.

## Snapshots

A snapshot is a named, dated copy of one item's text, kept in the project.

- **Take Snapshot** (<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd>, or in the inspector) before a
  big change.
- **Compare** opens the snapshot beside the current text, word by word: added words underlined,
  removed words struck through, with a count of each.
- **Restore** puts the snapshot back. Your current text is kept as a snapshot first, so nothing is
  lost.

Replace All across the project takes a snapshot of every document it changes, automatically.

## Revision passes

When you start a round of revision, run **Start Revision Pass** in the command palette. The pass
gets the next color, and from then on what you type or paste shows in that color.

- **Revision Mode** (<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>E</Kbd>) turns marking off and on.
- **Clear Revision Marks** takes one pass's marks, or all of them, off the text and leaves every
  word.

The marks are saved with the text and are never exported.

## Drafts

View › **Drafts** lists the draft you are writing and the drafts you have kept. Notes, research
and the story bible are shared by all drafts; only the manuscript changes.

- **New Draft** keeps the manuscript exactly as it is now under its current name ("First Draft"),
  and you carry on writing the new one ("Second Draft") in the binder.
- **Compare** shows a kept draft beside the one you are writing, chapter by chapter and scene by
  scene: what was added, cut or changed, with the words.
- **Write This** makes a kept draft the one you write again. The other is kept in its place.
- **Export This** chooses the draft that File › Export writes, and that Wordsmith Book typesets.

If you [import a set of documents](./import-and-export.mdx#a-set-of-documents) that holds several
drafts, the latest becomes the one you write and the earlier ones are kept as drafts.
