Wordsmith Book is for the last step of making a book. You bring a finished manuscript, choose a
book design, set the trim size, chapter openings and front and back matter, and get a print-ready
PDF for a paperback or hardcover and an EPUB for ebook stores. Every word is checked against your
source, so nothing you wrote is lost or changed without your say.

Book is for self-publishing authors first, and for small presses that make several books the same
way. It is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0), made by The James C. J. Foundation, for macOS,
Windows and Linux.

## Where it stands

<Callout type="warn" title="In development">
  Book is being built now and is not ready for real books yet. There are no installers, and you
  can't export a PDF or an EPUB from the app yet. If you want to look at it early, you can build
  it from source: [Getting started](./getting-started.mdx) shows how.
</Callout>

Book is younger than Wordsmith Script and Wordsmith Manuscript. Much of the work so far is the
part you don't see: reading your manuscript, typesetting the pages and writing the ebook. The
window that puts it all in your hands comes later. So in this section each feature is marked:

- <Status value="available" /> works in the app window today.
- <Status value="in-progress" /> is built and tested inside Book, but you can't reach it from the
  window yet.
- <Status value="planned" /> is not built yet.

## What you can do today

These work in the app window now. <Status value="available" />

- **Open a book project** and see its outline: front matter, parts, chapters and back matter.
- **Link a Wordsmith Manuscript project.** Book reads it and never changes it. When the writer
  changes the manuscript, Book notices.
- **Fix a typo** in a paragraph. For a linked manuscript, the fix is offered back to the writer to
  accept or reject in Manuscript.
- **Arrange front and back matter** by dragging it in the outline.
- **Choose a book design** from six, filtered by genre and mood, with a preview of your own first
  chapter.
- **Add editions** (paperback, hardcover, ebook) and give each its ISBN, which Book checks.
- **See what's missing** from your book's details before it can be published.
- **Review typographic cleanup** (curly quotes, dashes, ellipses) one change at a time, or keep the
  text exactly as written.
- **Keep your work safe** with auto-save, crash recovery and backups.

## Built, but not in the window yet

These are working and tested inside Book, and are waiting for the screens that use them.
<Status value="in-progress" />

- Importing a Word (DOCX), OpenDocument (ODT), RTF, Markdown or plain-text file, with chapters and
  scene breaks found for you and every word checked.
- Typesetting the whole book for print: trims, mirrored margins, running heads, page numbers,
  chapter openings, drop caps and scene breaks.
- Writing the EPUB: navigation, accessibility information, embedded fonts and the cover.
- Chapter number styles, subtitles and epigraphs, mixing designs and saving your own.

## Coming later

Not built yet. <Status value="planned" />

- Print and ebook previews.
- Preflight: checking the files against print and ebook rules before you upload them.
- Exporting the print PDF and the EPUB from the app.
- The command line for building books from a script.
- A cover template, footnotes, hardcover settings and more trims.

[What's next](./whats-next.mdx) has the whole plan.
