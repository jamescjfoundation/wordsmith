About

Built for writers, in James’ name.

Wordsmith is made by Wordsmith & Ink, a project of The James C. J. Foundation. Its goal is simple: give creatives the tools they need to create, free, on any computer, for good.

James

A writer with a world of stories.

James C. J. wrote novels, plays, screenplays, TV pilots, songs and poems, and built whole worlds behind them. The James C. J. Foundation keeps his work alive and is being built to support emerging writers in his name.

Wordsmith is one way the Foundation does that. Writing software is often expensive, closed, or tied to one kind of computer. These apps are none of those things, and because they're open source, they can't be taken away.

Visit jamescj.org

What we believe

Tools that belong to the writer.

01

Free, for good

No price, no subscription, no premium tier. Every feature is for every writer.

02

Your files are yours

Plain, open file formats you can read in any text editor, saved on your own computer.

03

No tracking

No accounts, no telemetry, no ads. Your words never leave your computer unless you send them.

04

Open source

Anyone can read the code, fix it, translate it and help decide where it goes next.

Wordsmith & Ink

The house behind the apps.

Wordsmith & Ink is the name the Foundation makes its writing tools under. Each app types its own name in Courier Prime with a cursor of its own: a brass bar for Script, a stack of pages for Manuscript, a ribbon bookmark for Book. The house itself is one color, ink on paper, with a drop of ink falling from its cursor.
