Each app works on its own. Where two of them meet, they share files rather than copying text
between them, so you never end up with two versions of the same chapter.

## Script files in a Manuscript project

A Manuscript project can hold Wordsmith Script files (`.wss`), next to your chapters. That's handy
for a screenplay adaptation of your novel, or a book with a script in it.

- Manuscript shows a script read-only, with its word and scene counts. You can give it a synopsis,
  a label and a status like any other item.
- To change it, open it in Wordsmith Script from Manuscript. When Script saves, the changes show
  in Manuscript.
- Screenplays are not part of the manuscript's exports.

See [Manuscript: The binder](../manuscript/binder.mdx#screenplays).

## From Manuscript to Book

When your novel is finished, Wordsmith Book turns it into a print book and an ebook.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Book links to your manuscript

In Book, make a new book **from a Manuscript project**: the project folder, or a packed `.wsmz`
file. Book reads the manuscript's sections, their roles and the text, and **never writes to it**,
so your manuscript stays exactly as you left it. In Manuscript, the **Book package** export makes
the one-file form to hand over.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep writing, if you need to

When the manuscript changes, Book notices the next time you come back to it, reads it again and
says which sections changed. The design and each section's settings in Book stay as they were.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fixes are offered back

If you fix a typo in Book, Book does not change your manuscript. It offers the fix to the writer.
In Manuscript, the status bar shows how many fixes wait; **Offered Fixes** lists each one with the
words before and after, and you accept or reject it. Until then Book's own builds use the fix.

</Step>
</Steps>

[Book: Working with Wordsmith Manuscript](../book/manuscript.mdx) has Book's side, and
[Manuscript: Exporting](../manuscript/export.mdx) the Manuscript side.

## Without Manuscript

Book does not need Manuscript. It imports a Word, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown, plain text or EPUB
file into a source of the book's own, and shows you what it found before anything is made. See
[Book: Importing a manuscript](../book/import.mdx).

## What is not connected

- **Script and Book** don't work with each other. Book is for prose books.
- **The story bible** belongs to Manuscript. Books of a series can share one bible; Script does
  not read it.
