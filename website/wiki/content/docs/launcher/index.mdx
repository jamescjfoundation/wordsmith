Wordsmith Launcher is one small program for the three Wordsmith apps: Wordsmith Script,
Wordsmith Manuscript and Wordsmith Book. It shows which of them are on your computer and at
which version, installs the ones you want, updates them, opens them and removes them. Its
interface is in English.

1. [Installing and updating the launcher](./installing.mdx): the download for your computer, how to open it, and how it keeps itself up to date.
2. [The apps](./apps.mdx): the list, installing an app, updating it, opening it and removing it, and where apps go on your computer.
3. [How a download is checked](./checking-a-download.mdx): what the launcher compares before it opens a file, and how to check the launcher's own download.
4. [The command line](./command-line.mdx): the same work from a terminal.
5. [What the launcher sends](./privacy.mdx): the requests it makes, and how to turn the automatic ones off.
6. [When something goes wrong](./problems.mdx): the messages the launcher shows, and what to do.
