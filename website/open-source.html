Early days

The road to 1.0.

We're at the start. The apps work, but they're young: no installers yet, and plenty still to build. The hope is to reach a solid 1.0 for each app, and to grow a community that carries the work forward from there.

Get involved

There's a place for you.

You don't need to write code to help. Everything happens in the open on GitHub.

Watch and star

Watch the repositories to hear about the first releases. Stars help other writers find the project.

Try it and tell us

Build an app from source, write something real in it, and open an issue for anything that gets in your way.

Write code

The apps are written in Rust. Each repository's contributing guide explains how changes are made and checked.

Translate

Standing on shoulders

Built on great open-source work.

The apps share one base, open-source-core, which is based on WordCraft by the ArtCraft team and uses craft-libs, egui, HarfRust, Vello and many other open-source projects. Every app credits what it uses in its README and NOTICE files, and fixes we make to those projects are offered back.

Privacy

Your writing stays with you.

The apps send no telemetry and have no accounts. Your writing, notes and file names are never sent anywhere. The only request an app makes on its own is a check for updates with GitHub, which you can turn off.
