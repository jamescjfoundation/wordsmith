Each app works on its own. Where two of them meet, they share files rather than copying text
between them, so you never end up with two versions of the same chapter.

## Script files in a Manuscript project

A Manuscript project can hold Wordsmith Script files (`.wss`) in its binder, next to your
chapters. That's handy for a screenplay adaptation of your novel, or a script you're writing
alongside it.

- Manuscript shows a script read-only, laid out as a script.
- To edit it, open it in Wordsmith Script. Your changes show up in Manuscript next time it reads
  the file.

See [Manuscript: The binder](../manuscript/binder.mdx).

## From Manuscript to Book

<Status value="in-progress" />

When your novel is finished, Wordsmith Book turns it into a print book and an ebook. Book is still
being built; this is how it works so far.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Book links to your manuscript

A book project points at your Manuscript project (the `.wsm` folder, or a packed `.wsmz` file).
Book **reads** it and **never writes to it**, so your manuscript stays exactly as you left it.
In Manuscript, **Export › Book package** makes a `.wsmz` file to hand to Book.

</Step>
<Step>

### Book offers fixes back

If Book finds something to fix in the text, it doesn't change your manuscript. It writes the
suggestion to a small file next to your project (`My Novel.wsm-offers.json`).

</Step>
<Step>

### You decide in Manuscript

Manuscript shows the suggestions in its **Offered fixes** list. Accept the ones you want and
reject the rest. The manuscript stays the one true copy of your words.

</Step>
</Steps>

Book can't yet start a new book from your manuscript in its window, or export the finished PDF
and EPUB. [Book: Bringing your book in](../book/manuscript.mdx) explains what works
today and [Manuscript: Sending to Book](../manuscript/export.mdx) covers the Manuscript
side.

## Not connected yet

- **Script and Book** don't work with each other; Book is for prose books.
- **Sharing a story bible with Script:** using one set of characters and places across a novel and
  its screenplay is <Status value="planned" />.
