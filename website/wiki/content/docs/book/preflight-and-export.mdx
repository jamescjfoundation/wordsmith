The last steps of making a book are seeing it, checking it and making the files you upload.
These are the next things to be built in Wordsmith Book, so most of this page describes what is
planned.

## Print preview

<Status value="planned" />

Two-page spreads of the typeset book, as they will be printed: left-hand pages on the left,
right-hand pages on the right, and the first page alone on the right. Guides show the trim and
the margins, and can be turned off. Clicking a page opens its chapter, and a chapter can jump to
its first page. The preview builds in the background, so the window never waits for it, and it
updates when you change a setting or the design.

## Ebook preview

<Status value="planned" />

A chapter shown as the ebook will look at phone, e-reader and tablet sizes, with switches for
larger text and a dark page. **Open in a reading app** hands the finished EPUB to the reading
app on your computer.

## Preflight

<Status value="planned" />

**Preflight** checks your book before you upload it, so a store or print service doesn't send it
back. It checks the finished files themselves, not your settings, and lists each problem in plain
words with a link to its page or chapter. Each problem is marked:

- **Blocks export**: must be fixed first.
- **Warning**: worth a look.
- **Note**: for your information.

For the print PDF, preflight will check the margins against the page count, that every font is
embedded and allowed to be, that nothing is smaller than 7 pt, that pictures are at least 300 dpi,
blank-page runs, the page count, and that every word matches your source.

For the EPUB, it will check the package, the contents and links, that every picture has a
description, the accessibility information, and that every word matches your source. If you have
the standard EPUB validator installed, Book will run it too. If you don't, that is a note, not a
failure.

It will also list the details still missing, such as an ISBN, and any fixes still waiting for
the writer to accept in Manuscript.

Some of these checks already run inside Book while it is being built. They come to you with the
**Preflight** panel. <Status value="in-progress" />

## Export

<Status value="planned" />

The export page will list your editions and what to make for each: the print PDF, the EPUB, and
a **project bundle**. Before writing any file, Book checks the words once more, and if the check
fails it writes nothing.

The project bundle is the whole book in one file: the book, its designs, any fonts you added, the
pictures, and a copy of the linked Manuscript project. It opens on another computer just as it is.

The same book always gives the same files, byte for byte, so a rebuild months later matches what
you published.

## The command line

<Status value="planned" />

For small presses that build many books the same way, `book-cli` will build an edition (or all of
them), run preflight, check the words and turn a file into a book project, with exit codes for
scripts.
