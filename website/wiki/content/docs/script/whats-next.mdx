Wordsmith Script is pre-release software. Everything on this page is **not
available yet**. Plans can change, and nothing here has a date.

## The first release

- <Status value="planned" /> **Installers** for macOS, Windows and Linux, so you no longer need to build the app from source.

## Being considered

These are candidates for later versions, in no particular order.

- <Status value="planned" /> **Dark colors in the PDF**: today PDFs are always black on white, whatever the theme.
- <Status value="planned" /> **Pictures** in the script and in story board beats.
- <Status value="planned" /> **Alternate lines**: trying another version of a line without losing the first.
- <Status value="planned" /> **Replies to notes**, for conversations in the margin.
- <Status value="planned" /> **Read-aloud**: hearing the script read to you.

## Things it can't do yet

Some smaller gaps you may run into today:

- Pages are US Letter only; **A4 paper** is not available yet, and there are no
  presets for tighter or looser line spacing.
- **Spelling** is English only, with no thesaurus.
- **Pasting** brings in plain text, so element types don't come with it.
- **Imports** don't keep bold, italic and underline, and scanned PDFs can't be
  read.
- **Tracked changes** aren't seen by spelling, find and exports until accepted.
- The second speech of **dual dialogue** is edited in the normal or draft view,
  not the page view.
- The ribbon's tabs and groups can't be rearranged.
- Double-clicking a script in the Finder or File Explorer doesn't open it in the
  app yet; open it from the app or name it on the command line.
- Only Linux installs an update by itself; on macOS and Windows the update
  check opens the download page.
- Opening the same script twice lets both copies save over each other, so keep
  one window per script.

## Left out on purpose

Some things are out of scope, so the app stays simple and your work stays on
your computer:

- **Proprietary file formats.** Scripts move in and out through Fountain, the
  `.wss` format, PDF, RTF and plain text. A closed format could be added only
  once its description is published openly.
- **Real-time collaboration** and **cloud sync.** Your files are yours: keep them
  wherever you like.
- **AI writing features.**
- **Production breakdown tagging** and scheduling export.
