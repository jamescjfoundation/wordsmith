A **design** decides how your book looks: the typeface and size of the text, how chapters open,
how scene breaks are marked, and what goes in the running heads. One design covers both the print
book and the ebook, so the two match.

## Choosing a design

<Status value="available" />

Open the **Designs** panel. Each design shows a short description and a preview of your own
book's first chapter: its title and opening lines in that design's sizes and style. Narrow the
list with the **Genre** and **Mood** filters, then choose **Choose** beside the one you want. The
design in use is marked "(in use)". Changing the design is one step you can undo.

<Callout type="info">
  Today the preview is a quick sketch of the chapter's opening, not the printed page. A preview of
  the real typeset first page is planned. <Status value="planned" />
</Callout>

## The six designs

Every design follows the same good habits: justified text with a first-line indent and no space
between paragraphs, no indent on the first paragraph of a chapter or after a scene break, at
least two lines of a paragraph on each side of a page break, hyphenation for the book's language,
and no running heads on chapter openings or blank pages.

| Design | For | Chapter openings | Scene breaks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** | Classics, literary and historical fiction | On a right-hand page, a quarter of the way down; the number in small capitals and an ornament; the first words in small capitals | An ornament |
| **Literary** | Literary and contemporary fiction | On a right-hand page, a third of the way down, set to the left; a plain number and title | A blank line, the next scene starting in small capitals |
| **Romance** | Romance, historical and women's fiction | On a right-hand page; a large display title, an ornament and a three-line drop cap | An ornament |
| **Thriller** | Thrillers, mysteries, crime and science fiction | On a right-hand page, set to the left; a big sans-serif number and the title in capitals | Three asterisks |
| **Fantasy** | Fantasy, historical and young adult | On a right-hand page; display capitals, ornaments and a three-line drop cap | Three ornaments |
| **Non-fiction** | Non-fiction, memoir and essays | On any page, set to the left; a sans-serif number and title | Three asterisks |

Running heads differ too. Classic, Romance and Fantasy put the author on the left page and the
title on the right, in small capitals, with the page number at the foot. Literary, Thriller and
Non-fiction put the title on the left and the chapter on the right, with the page number at the
top.

The designs use typefaces with open licenses that come with Book, so there is nothing to buy or
install. Every typeface a design uses can be embedded in a PDF and an ebook, and has every
ornament the design prints.

## Mixing designs

<Status value="in-progress" />

You can take parts of your book from different designs: the chapter openings from Fantasy, say,
with the body text and scene breaks from Literary. The parts you can mix are the **body text**,
the **chapter opening**, the **scene breaks** and the **running heads**. Every pair of designs mixes
into a working design.

This is built and saved with the book; the controls for it in the window are still to come.

## Your own designs

<Status value="in-progress" />

A design you have changed or mixed can be saved as your own, under its own name, inside the book
project. You can also export a design as one small file and import it into another book, or give
it to someone else.

A design file is plain text, so it can be shared and kept with your other files. Book checks
every value when it reads one and explains any problem in plain words. A misspelled setting is
reported rather than quietly ignored.

As with mixing, this works inside Book and is waiting for its place in the window.
