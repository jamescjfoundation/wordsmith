Your writing stays with you. The Wordsmith apps have no accounts and send no telemetry: no usage
statistics, no crash reports, nothing about what you write or how. Your text, notes and file names
are never sent anywhere.

## The update check

One request leaves your computer without your asking: the check for a newer version. Script,
Manuscript and Book all make it, in the same way.

- **When:** each time the app starts, and when you choose **Check for Updates**.
- **Where to:** GitHub's API (`api.github.com`), for the list of that app's own releases. No other
  server is asked.
- **What is sent:** the request itself. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address. The request
  names the app and its version, as in `WordsmithBook/1.0.0`, and nothing else: nothing about
  you, your computer or your work.
- **What comes back:** the list of releases. If there is a newer one, the app tells you.
- **Downloads:** nothing is downloaded unless you choose to install the update. It then comes
  from that release on github.com.

If the check at start fails, for example because you are offline, the app says nothing and carries
on. A check you asked for says why it failed.

### Turning it off

Open **File › Preferences** and clear **Check for updates when the app starts**. The app then
sends nothing unless you check by hand.

The command-line tools that come with the apps never check for updates.

## Things you start yourself

Some features hand your work to another program or open something outside the app. They happen
only when you ask, and the program runs on your own computer.

- **Printing** hands the pages to your system's print service or PDF viewer.
- **A converter you installed** can be named in Manuscript's export, for a format Manuscript does
  not write.
- **Validators you installed** can be named in Book's Preferences; preflight then runs them on
  the ebook or the PDF.
- **Open in a Reading App** in Book hands the ebook to the reading app on your computer.
- **A web link** kept in a Manuscript project opens in your browser when you choose
  Open in Browser. Keeping the link downloads nothing.

Grammar hints in Manuscript are checked on your own computer; no text is sent anywhere.

## Your own history

Script's writing statistics and Manuscript's writing history are kept on your computer, beside
the app's preferences, never in a script or a project. They do not travel with a file you share.

## This website and wiki

wordsmithand.ink and this wiki don't use cookies, analytics or trackers, and the fonts are served
from the site itself, so no visitor data goes to a font service. The site is hosted on GitHub
Pages.
