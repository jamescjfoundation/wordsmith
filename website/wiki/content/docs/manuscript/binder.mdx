The binder on the left of the window holds everything in your project as a tree. It always has
five areas:

| Area | For |
| --- | --- |
| Manuscript | The book itself. This is what exports include. |
| Story Bible | Characters, places, objects, groups, lore and plot threads. See [The story bible](./story-bible.mdx). |
| Research | Research notes, earlier drafts, rough work, pictures, PDFs and web links. |
| Notes | Your plans and notes. |
| Trash | Deleted items, until you empty it. |

## Documents, folders and roles

Items are documents and folders. A document can hold other documents, so a chapter can be a
document with its scenes under it.

Every item in the Manuscript has a **role**: part, chapter, scene, front matter, back matter, or
none. Roles decide how exports title and number things, so you never type "Chapter 3" yourself.
A new item takes its role from where you put it: inside a part it is a chapter, inside a chapter
a scene. To change a role, right-click the item.

**Include in export** (right-click) leaves an item, and everything under it, out of exports
without deleting it. It shows dimmed in the binder.

## Adding, renaming and moving

- **New Document** (<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>N</Kbd>) adds a document after the
  selected one. **New Folder** is <Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>N</Kbd>.
- **Rename** with <Kbd>F2</Kbd> or a double-click on the title.
- **Drag** an item before, after or inside another. A line or a box shows where it will land.
- **With the keyboard**, <Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Up</Kbd> and <Kbd>Down</Kbd> move an
  item among its neighbours. <Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Right</Kbd> moves it into the
  item before it, and <Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Left</Kbd> moves it out of its folder.

Every move can be undone in one step. The binder, the corkboard and the outline always show the
same order.

## The Trash

**Move to Trash** (<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>Backspace</Kbd>) puts an item and its
children in the Trash. **Restore** puts it back where it came from.

**Empty Trash** asks first, telling you how many items and words will go. They are removed for
good once the project is saved.

## Bookmarks

<Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd> (or right-click › Bookmark) bookmarks the selected item. Its title
then sits in the row of bookmarks above the binder, one click away from anywhere in the project.
Press <Kbd>Mod</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd> again, or right-click › Remove Bookmark, to take it off.

## Collections

A collection is a list of items beside the binder. The items stay where they are.

- **Hand-picked**: right-click an item › Add to Collection, and choose a collection or start a
  new one.
- **Saved searches**: after a project search, Save Search keeps it as a collection that always
  shows what matches now.

When a project has collections, the menu above the binder switches between the binder and each
collection. × takes an item out of a hand-picked collection.

## Filters

Above the binder you can filter by label, status, keyword and point of view. Items that don't
match are hidden in the binder, the corkboard and the outline; their folders stay, so you can
still see where things are. Show All clears the filter.

## Pictures, PDFs and web links

Research can hold more than notes.

- **Add Image or PDF…** (right-click Research, or a folder in it): type the file's path. PNG,
  JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures and PDFs up to 64 MB are copied into the project, so they travel
  with it and are in its backups.
- **Add Web Link…** keeps a web address. Nothing is downloaded.

Select one to see it in the editor area. A picture fits the width. A PDF shows the text of each
page, and Open in Another App shows the pages themselves once the project is saved. A link shows
its address with Open in Browser.

## Screenplays

A project can hold screenplays, for an adaptation or a book with a script in it. Use Add Image or
PDF… and choose a Wordsmith Script file (`.wss`). It goes into the Manuscript as a screenplay
item, its file kept exactly as Wordsmith Script wrote it.

Select it to read it, with its word and scene counts. You can give it a synopsis, label and status
like any other item, and it shows on the corkboard and in the outline. To change it, choose Open
in Another App: it opens in Wordsmith Script, and when Script saves, the changes show in
Manuscript. Screenplays are not part of the manuscript's exports.
