The apps

One family, three desks.

Each app does one job well, and they work together. All three run on macOS, Windows and Linux, and keep your work as plain files on your own computer.

Write screenplays, TV episodes and stage plays, formatted as you type and ready for production.

How it works

Write → Finish → Publish.

One shared foundation means the apps look alike, work alike and pass your work along without copying and pasting.

  1. 1

    Script

    Write the screenplay, and keep it in your novel's project too.

  2. 2

    Manuscript

    Draft and revise the story, with characters and places in one bible.

  3. 3

    Book

    Turn the finished manuscript into a print book and an ebook.

The planned Wordsmith Launcher will bring all three together in one download.
